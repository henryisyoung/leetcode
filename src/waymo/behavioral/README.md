## Question → story cheat sheet

- **Biggest impact / most significant project** → AvatarAssetVersioning
- **Problem nobody assigned you / got an initiative funded** → AvatarAssetVersioning, AppealValidation
- **Had to give something up / deprioritize** → AvatarAssetVersioning (the only story with a real trade)
- **Got another team to adopt your work** → AvatarAssetVersioning, LatencyRequirementFailure
- **Disagree & commit / pushed back then aligned** → InExperienceCreation
- **Balance competing priorities / safety vs. business** → MarketplaceRemediation, BlackImageIncident
- **Challenge the status quo / reframe a problem** → MarketplaceRemediation, BlackImageIncident
- **Influence without authority** → BlackImageIncident, MarketplaceRemediation
- **Feedback you received & acted on** → MarketplaceRemediation (variant)
- **Ownership / went beyond your scope** → AppealValidation, BlackImageIncident
- **Earn trust** → AppealValidation (variant)
- **Lead a complex cross-functional initiative** → BlackImageIncident
- **Drive a project end-to-end across teams** → BlackImageIncident, AppealValidation
- **Adapt existing systems to new requirements** → InExperienceCreation
- **Project that failed / neutral outcome** → NeutralOutcomeProjectLearning
- **Product judgment / bad metric lesson** → NeutralOutcomeProjectLearning
- **Long-term thinking / extensibility lesson** → NeutralOutcomeProjectLearning
- **Requirement was met but users were unhappy** → LatencyRequirementFailure
- **Customer feedback changed your approach** → LatencyRequirementFailure

## Notes

- Every story ends with a **Waymo bridge** sentence to land it for that interview.
- Have 1-2 quantified results ready where possible (latency, refund volume, false-positive rate, bookings impact).
